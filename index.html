// ARCHITECTURE & PRINCIPLES

Designed for focus, speed & absolute privacy

Everything runs in memory and saves to your local browser storage. No accounts, no subscriptions, and zero remote tracking.

🛡️

Private by Design

No accounts, no tracking cookies, and no servers. The developer never sees or stores anything you create.

⚡

Instant & Offline-First

Bundled completely inside the extension, fonts included. Opens in its own app window from the toolbar, works without internet, and never lags.

✨

Neon Glass Aesthetic

High-contrast dark mode with neon accents, animated Bézier flow arrows, subtle glassmorphism, plus three more skins: Soft and Pink light themes and the dark, earthy Moss.

🧠

Integrated Thinking

Switch effortlessly between mind maps, Kanban, Gantt charts, rich notebooks, to-dos, meeting prep, people notes and the Link Pocket. Relate any item to any other, across every view, and keep every map, board, notebook and chart one click away in Collections.

📆

Unified Calendar & ToDay

One shared calendar, in a window or full screen, surfaces every dated Kanban card, Gantt task, to-do, meeting, time off and holiday, and ToDay lists what is due today. Never lose track of deadlines across views.

💾

Portable File Control

Single-click Save All writes your whole workspace to one JSON file, with automatic backups. Every view has its own Export: PNG images, standalone HTML, CSV, Markdown and JSON.

🧰

Built-in Data Tools

JSON Fixer repairs broken JSON and marks every fix. CSV Fixer cleans spreadsheet exports and warns about Excel pitfalls. Base64 / URL encodes, decodes and reads JWTs. QR Code turns a link or any text into a QR code to save as PNG or SVG. All on your device: nothing you paste is sent or saved.

🤖

Connect Your AI (optional)

Let your own AI app (Claude Desktop, Claude Code or another MCP app) read, search, add and change your maps, cards, notes, to-dos, meetings and people. It talks only to a small helper on your own computer and stays off until you turn it on. Mark any item “AI hidden” to keep it away from the AI; every change can be undone and deleting always asks you first. Your AI app handles what it reads under its own privacy terms.
