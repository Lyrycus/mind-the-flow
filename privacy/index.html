Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 (Version 1.0.4)

Mind the Flow is an offline-capable visual thinking suite containing mind maps, Kanban boards, Gantt charts, notebooks, to-dos, meeting prep, people notes, a Link Pocket of web links and data tools (JSON, CSV, Base64 / URL, QR codes), with an optional connection to your own AI app. It runs entirely inside your browser on your own device.

Summary: We collect zero personal data. We run no telemetry, no tracking analytics, and no remote databases. Everything you write stays strictly in your browser. The only thing that ever reaches the developer is anonymous feedback you choose to send.

What we collect

Nothing. Mind the Flow has no user accounts, no backend servers, no analytics, and no tracking cookies. The developer never receives, sees, transmits, or stores anything you create, including when you use Connect AI (below). The only exception is the optional, anonymous feedback form described below: the developer sees only the answers you choose to submit, with no email address or account attached.

Where your data lives

  • Local Storage: Everything you create (mind maps, Kanban boards, notebooks, to-dos, meetings, people notes, Link Pocket links, and custom settings) is stored securely in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Exports on Demand: When you use Save All, PNG, or HTML export, files are written exclusively to a folder or download location that you select on your computer.
  • Data Control: Uninstalling the extension clears its local storage. Use Save All beforehand if you wish to preserve a standalone backup of your projects.

Network requests

  • None by default: Everything the Chrome extension needs, including its fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono, distributed under the SIL Open Font License), is bundled inside the package. It functions 100% offline and initiates no outbound network connections unless you use one of the actions below.
  • HTML export: A view-only map you export links the Inter font from Google Fonts, so it is loaded (with your IP address, standard for web fonts) only when someone opens that exported HTML file in a web browser.
  • Add to Calendar: When you choose to click an "Add to calendar" action, Google Calendar opens in a new tab with that item's title, date, and description pre-filled. Nothing is transmitted until you click it, and saving the event is entirely up to you inside Google Calendar.
  • Send feedback: When you press 💬 Feedback (bottom right of the app), an anonymous Google Form loads inside that window (and is removed when you close it). It collects no email address and needs no sign-in. Your answers are sent to Google Forms only when you press Submit, and only those answers reach the developer. Google handles the form under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Uninstalling: When you remove the extension, Chrome opens our feedback page with the same anonymous form; its address names the extension version. Filling it in is entirely optional.
  • External links: Any web hyperlinks you manually insert into cards, notes or the Link Pocket open only when you intentionally click them. The Link Pocket never fetches a page, a title or an icon by itself, and QR codes are drawn on your device.
  • Connect AI (off unless you turn it on): Lets an AI app you use (such as Claude Desktop or Claude Code) work with your maps, boards, notes, to-dos, meetings and people. Mind the Flow then talks only to a small helper program on your own computer (at 127.0.0.1), which your AI app starts, and only after you pair them with a code. Mind the Flow sends nothing to the internet for this. Your AI app can read and change your items when it asks for them, and it sends what it reads to its own AI provider, under that provider's privacy policy; that never passes through the developer. To keep an item away from it, click AI visible in the item's details so it shows AI hidden (the item gets the tag private): your AI app can then never read, find, change or delete it. Each change can be undone, and deleting always asks you first in the app. Turning Connect AI off (menu → Settings → Connect AI…) or uninstalling stops it at once.

No other third party receives any data from Mind the Flow. With Connect AI on, your own AI app receives what it asks for, as described above.

Limited Use Disclosure

The use of information received from Chrome APIs by Mind the Flow adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is never sold, never transferred to data brokers, never used for advertising, profiling, or creditworthiness evaluation, and is never inspected by anyone other than you.

Extension Permissions

  • notifications: Used solely to display local due-date reminders for your Kanban cards and scheduled to-dos while the application is active in your browser. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in the settings menu.
  • storage: Used solely to remember the size and position of the application window on your device, so it reopens where you left it.
  • http://127.0.0.1/* (optional): Asked for only when you turn on Connect AI, so the app can talk to the helper program on your own computer. It is never used for anything else.

Contact & Support

If you have questions, feedback, or need clarification regarding this policy:

  • Send anonymous feedback with the app's 💬 Feedback button (bottom right) or on our feedback page.
  • Contact the developer via the official support contact email listed on the Chrome Web Store item listing.
